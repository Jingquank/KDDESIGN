# DesignDelta is stills first, under a budget, and its Record is a decision log without images

The full run on a product site's developer page published 197 files and 32.7 MB against a budget of 60 and 8. The 24 stills weighed 1.2 MB; the other 29 MB was Real: one Real Change ships the app's chunks and every media file its section loads, a fixed cost that no amount of scoping removes. And the part worth keeping, the Record, carried thumbnails that a future agent cannot read and the commits can always regenerate.

So every side of every Change now has stills, whatever its Fidelity, and Fidelity only decides what Round 1 adds on top. The ladder is Screenshot (stills, plus preview links when the project has preview deployments, which let a reviewer operate the real thing for zero bytes), then Rebuilt (a frame of only the piece, on the side that responds), then Real (only when Rebuilt cannot be faithful, such as a WebGL stage). `validate.py` fails a publish over 8 MB, so the budget is a check, not a hope (the file count is not budgeted: it grows by four stills per Change and costs nothing); the agent walks down the ladder, and only the user raises the budget. Final is static: stills and links, with the Round 1 frames dropped from the Artifact. Stills are AVIF at most 960px wide, encoded by one script (`viewer/still.py`), because a side-by-side pane never shows more than about 600 CSS px; on the devpage run this halved the Final to 1.1 MB. Before and After are always side by side; the slider was dropped. Rebuilt frames are built from the built page by two tools (`snapshot.js` captures every state a control reaches and the CSS rules used; `rebuilt.py` writes the frames), and the frames of one state share one pruned stylesheet: on devpage the site's 108 KB of CSS shipped as 71 KB once instead of six times.

The Record follows Vercel's exemplars and decision records (`pr-{name}.md`; Decision, Rationale, Evidence, good and bad example; rules with stable ids that stay pending until a person accepts them): per Change, the decision, why, what to repeat, what to avoid, each with its source, a pending rule candidate when a source states one, and evidence as links. No images; Vercel keeps visuals behind links too. This supersedes the Real-or-Rebuilt-for-operated rule of ADR 0005 and the thumbnails of ADR 0004.
