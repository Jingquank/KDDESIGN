# design-morph sounds by one fixed Cue map, loud where the eye goes

A Morph film now plays interaction sounds from cuelume. Who decides which motion makes which sound was the open question: the agent per film, or the skill once. Letting the agent choose drifts from film to film and adds one more thing to review, so `engine/sound.js` holds one Cue map, read straight from the scene data the engine already has, and a film can only silence a few elements (`sound.mute`, each with its reason) or pick another theme when the user asks. The map came out of five prototype rounds on the Spotify to Apple Music film, the user listening to each: a sound per Chapter and a single count per stagger were too far from the picture, and a sound per element was too even. What held up was salience: the more of the stage a motion covers, the farther it travels and the more it resizes, the louder and lower it sounds, and the faintest fifth stay silent; staggers fall as rain that never repeats itself, the Guides speak softly, the skin turn is the one strong beat, the title card and the act's end are silent, cues that crowd within 150 ms are turned down by the square root of their number, and no cue ever plays the same as its last play. Bubble is the default theme.

Two costs come with it. cuelume exposes pitch only through a cue's direction, key and input, so the bundled copy in `engine/cuelume.js` carries a small patch (`play(name, { pitch })`, a wider pitch clamp), documented in its header; an upstream pitch option would let it go. And sound needs a gesture, so a film with sound now opens paused, and its first Play starts both. The MP4 stays silent until the cues are rendered offline into it. Not chosen: per-film sound design by the agent, a sound per Chapter, and CDN loading, which the Artifact frame blocks.
