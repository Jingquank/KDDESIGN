# design-morph stands alone

A film looks like a natural follow-up to a Design Changelog, so it is tempting to have `/design-delta` offer one, or the Nudge suggest it when a redesign settles. Neither does. A film is made for an audience (a review, a launch, a team update), by choice and rarely, and it costs a browser session of minutes; the Nudge exists to keep the rule loop fed and should not compete with it. `/design-morph` writes nothing into the repo, so it has nothing to give `/design-lessons` either. It runs only by hand, and `/design-delta` does not mention it.
