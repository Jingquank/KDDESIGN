# design-morph measures and shoots its Acts in parallel

Each Act of a Morph film is one page pair, and two steps of a run touch each Act alone: measuring its Before and After pages (step 4), and the first text check and stills of its stretch of the film (step 9). With 2 or more Acts being written, one measurer per Act measures its pages in a tab of its own, and later one shooter per Act runs `texts` and `stills` over its Act's seconds against the one server. The main agent keeps the worktrees, the dev servers, the assets, the mapping, the scene data, every build, every fix and every reshoot.

Not chosen: splitting the mapping or the scene writing per Act, since a Pair the agent is unsure of is a question for the user, which a subagent cannot ask, and the lessons are applied while writing by the one agent that holds them all. Splitting the check-and-fix loop of step 8, since `build.mjs` and `check.mjs` work on one film folder and two agents fixing it at once would rebuild over each other. A second reviewer per Act, since ADR 0026 keeps one reviewer for every round so its bar stays the same.
