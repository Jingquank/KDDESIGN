# design-delta and design-morph read a page only once it is at rest, and design-delta's stills get a fresh eye

Stageshow counts an empty, late or gapped capture as inconclusive, never as a pass, and its reviewer judges only frames. KDDESIGN's two skills that read live pages did neither for their input. design-delta took each Before and After as soon as the page loaded, and design-morph measured every box the same way. Yet a font still loading, an image still empty or an entrance still moving gives the wrong still and the wrong boxes, and nothing downstream can tell. design-delta also judged from code which diffs are visible, and nobody checked that a still showed its Change, though those stills become the Bad and Good examples of the rules `/design-lessons` drafts.

So both now ask the page first whether it is at rest: design-delta with `viewer/ready.js`, design-morph inside `tools/measure.js`. At rest means the fonts are loaded and the product's own are among them, images in view are decoded, no animation with an end is moving anything, nothing is busy, and the layout holds still for 300 ms. A page that never comes to rest is used as it stands and named at the hand-over. `validate.py` warns when a Screenshot Change's Before and After are within 32 pixels of each other: the state was not forced, the crop is wrong, or the wrong build was captured. Once its stills are taken, design-delta gives a reviewer each Change's title, description and stills, and nothing else, and asks it to find the Change in them. What it cannot find is recaptured or reworded once, then handed over.

Not chosen: refusing to publish while a side is not at rest, since one stuck spinner would hold back a whole Changelog and naming it is enough. Not a share-of-pixels bar for equal stills either: one recoloured word on a tall still is under 0.1%. And no self-check for `/crazy-prototype`, whose worth is speed, quantity and range, not finish.
