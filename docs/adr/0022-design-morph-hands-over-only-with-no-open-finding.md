# design-morph hands over only when the checker is clean and the frames have been looked at

On the first film, "every in-between frame must look sound" was checked by rendering frames and looking, round after round, and the published film still had collisions nobody saw at review speed: when its Library act was replayed through the new checker it reported 24, among them tiles flying through each other, the credits bolt crossing the count it swaps places with, and the sidebar growing into the canvas for a few frames. So the check is now code: `check.mjs` steps each act 200 times through the engine's own state and reports overlap between siblings, crossing a container's or a pane's edge, leftovers at the end, and time that steps backwards, including the player clock that once picked `ACTS[-1]`.

Geometry cannot see color, so the visual pass stays: contact sheets of rendered frames, looked at once the checker is clean. That pass found the one fault the checker could not, sidebar labels fading into a pane that had not yet turned, and it became an engine rule for every film (text turns its skin with its pane). The run keeps a checklist and may not hand over, publish a hand-over version, silence a finding with an exemption, or edit the engine while a line is open; SKILL.md names these early stops. Not chosen: a looser checker that only flags large overlaps, which would have passed every collision above.
